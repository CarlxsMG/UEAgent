# Code of conduct

UEAgent is a place to build and to learn. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests or
discussions, is expected to:

- be respectful and constructive, and assume good intent;
- keep criticism on the work, never on the person;
- never harass, insult or discriminate against anyone.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions that break these rules, and block whoever keeps
breaking them. To report a problem, contact the maintainer at <https://github.com/CarlxsMG>.
